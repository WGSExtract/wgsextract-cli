What’s a haplogroup?

A haplogroup is a named lineage-tree branch defined by shared variants. Alex’s mtDNA follows her direct maternal line: her mother, her maternal grandmother and so on. The path shows nested branches; name length alone does not date a branch.

Haplogroups describe one direct lineage, not overall ancestry. A chip may miss defining markers, and sharing a branch does not prove a recent relationship. Learn the basics: haplogroups and their limits.

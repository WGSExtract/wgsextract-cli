What are these files?

Each raw-data file lists one marker per line with Helen’s called genotype from her sequencing file, formatted to match what that company’s own test produces.

The GEDmatch .zip combines marker lists from several chips so more positions overlap when comparing kits. Each site decides which formats it accepts, and blank calls or small marker overlap can limit matches. Check the site’s upload rules and privacy terms before uploading.

What to do next

GEDmatch

  1. Sign in at gedmatch.com, or make a free account.
  2. Choose Upload your DNA files, then Generic upload.
  3. Pick helen-mcdonald_CombinedKit.zip as it is; don’t unzip it.
  4. One-to-many matches appear in 1 to 3 days, once GEDmatch has processed the kit.

MyHeritage

  1. Sign in at myheritage.com.
  2. Open DNA, then Upload DNA data.
  3. Pick helen-mcdonald_MyHeritage_V2.csv.gz.
  4. Matches and an ethnicity estimate appear in about 2 to 4 weeks.

Uploading shares Helen’s genotypes with that site. Read its privacy terms first; nothing leaves this Mac until you upload.

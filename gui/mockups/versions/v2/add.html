Which files can I add?
BAM or CRAM
Read sequences, quality scores and their alignment records against a reference. Keep these to inspect evidence or call variants again; they cannot restore reads or quality detail the lab already discarded.
FASTQ
Raw reads that haven’t been aligned yet, usually two files. They’re aligned first, which takes a few hours.
VCF
Variant Call Format: coordinates, alleles and called genotypes, usually at variant sites only. Useful for annotation and some exports; missing sites are not automatically reference.
Raw data
The text file from 23andMe, AncestryDNA, MyHeritage and similar tests: about 600,000–700,000 markers.

Labs and testing companies offer these as downloads in your account. Keep the BAM or CRAM if you have it: a VCF or a raw-data file can be made from it, but not the other way round.

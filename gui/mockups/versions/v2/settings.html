Privacy

Default operations stay offline. Public data is downloaded only when you ask:

  • reference genomes and the extra data for annotation
  • example genomes, when you ask to try one
  • new versions of WGS Extract, when you choose to update

Public downloads do not send your genome. ACMG per-run site lookups are a separate opt-in: they send variant positions to Google Cloud Storage and Ensembl. Coming remote viewing has its own destination and transfer disclosure. Phone access is a mockup proposal, not the production local viewer endpoint.

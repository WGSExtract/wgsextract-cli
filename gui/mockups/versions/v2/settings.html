Privacy

Your genome files never leave this computer. WGS Extract goes online only for downloads you start:

  • reference genomes and the extra data for annotation
  • example genomes, when you ask to try one
  • new versions of WGS Extract, when you choose to update

Nothing about your files is sent with these requests. Phone access stays on your local network; see Phone access.

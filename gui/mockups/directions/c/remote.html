How it stays private

  • Encrypted. The QR code carries this computer’s certificate fingerprint, so the phone knows it’s talking to this Mac.
  • Your local network only. Away from home, use a private network such as Tailscale; never open a port on your router.
  • One key per device; remove a phone and its key stops working.
  • Turns itself off after 7 days with no paired device connecting.

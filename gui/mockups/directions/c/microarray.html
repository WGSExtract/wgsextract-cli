Make DNA-test files

Turn a whole genome into the raw-data files that DNA-testing sites accept, to find relatives in their databases. Pick where you’ll use them; WGS Extract picks the right chip versions.

From

Where will you use them?

23andMe and AncestryDNA don’t take uploads. Their formats are for other tools that read those files. Each file has the markers of that company’s chip, called from your genome; markers your genome doesn’t cover are left blank, as on a real kit.

You’ll get 2 files

  • GEDmatch combined kit · ≈ 2 M markers
  • MyHeritage v2 · ≈ 610 k markers
  • 31× coverage: about 99.6% of markers will be called
  • Reference hs38 and the microarray targets are installed
  • About 12 minutes · 40 MB in HG002/microarray/

Server: POST /v1/ops/microarray with formats: "all,myheritage_v2"; the combined kit is always written.

Changes: retain only chrY intervals; omit the original mitochondrial intervals.
Coordinates are copied unchanged: BED zero-based, half-open.
hg19.bed is the original hg37 BED (GRCh37 chrY, also UCSC hg19 chrY).
t2t.bed (T2T-CHM13v2.0, UCSC hs1 chrY) is derived from hg38.bed (SHA256
b887a2b25917a4f11e5534f7e469c6143177cb5cdb53dac27ade6c7307e26128) by
scripts/build_poz_t2t.py with the UCSC hg38ToHs1 liftOver chain
https://hgdownload.soe.ucsc.edu/goldenPath/hg38/liftOver/hg38ToHs1.over.chain.gz
SHA256 926d4583eda8f90c88434293859b06906573f2469630e898b8b475ac8751327a.
Each region is cut to the chain's aligned chrY-to-chrY blocks (T2T sequence
absent from GRCh38 is not added; 880 bp landing on chrX are dropped), then
pieces less than 100 bp apart are joined: 11,595,737 of 11,654,787 bp lift.
t2t.bed SHA256:
c372f3050487404276fcb5e1b8f5f8719185ed9d32762ff555ba28b7f17f1efb
The chain is a build-time input only; it is not redistributed or fetched at
runtime (UCSC terms: https://hgdownload.soe.ucsc.edu/goldenPath/hg38/liftOver/README.txt).
Contig naming is adapted at runtime to the input's chrY/Y name.

Original reference/00README.txt credits David Vance's HG38 spreadsheet.
WGS Extract v4 manual section "To Poz BAM" describes this union as approximately
12 million bases of regions used in phylogenetic/age analyses; it is NOT all
stable Y SNP sites, an exome capture kit, or a BigY capture BED.
https://github.com/theontho/WGSExtractRepo/blob/f45c60e/docs/WGS_Extract_Manual_Alphav4.md
